1. The Application

Dad Health (‘Licensed Application’) is a mobile application created for iOS and Android devices (‘Devices’). Dad Health is a personal mental health, fitness, nutrition and parenting companion built specifically for dads. Key features include:

  • Daily mental health check-in and mood tracking
  • Dad Health Score — a composite wellbeing score across Mind, Body and Bond
  • Fitness tracking, workout sessions, manual activity logging and TDEE calculator
  • Meal planning and nutrition guidance
  • Bond tracking, quality-time logging, Present Dad Mode and parenting support
  • Dad Days AI-powered local activity search
  • Therapist directory filtered for dad-friendly slots
  • Anonymous community feed and Dad Circles

Dad Health is a wellness tool and not a clinical mental health service. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact the emergency services on 999, the Samaritans on 116 123 (free, 24/7), or CALM on 0800 58 58 58 (5pm–midnight daily).
