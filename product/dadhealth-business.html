A benefit dads will actually use

Most wellbeing perks are built for everyone, so many men never touch them. Dad Health speaks to fathers directly, in a few minutes a day.

Mind

Breathing resets, guided reflection, daily check-ins and a journal, with support one tap away when a dad needs it.

Body

Workouts built around his time and equipment, and any activity he already does can be logged, from CrossFit to a walk.

Bond

Ideas for days out, cooking together and Present Dad Mode: sixty minutes, phone down, just him and his kids.

Every dad gets a Dad Health Score across the three pillars, a streak to keep him coming back, and the full Pro experience, paid for by you.

Real insight. Zero snooping.

Dads only use a tool like this if they trust it. So your team's data stays theirs, and you get the picture without the detail.

  • Team-level averages for Mind, Body and Bond
  • Seats used and engagement over time
  • Month-by-month trends you can share with HR and leadership
  • Nothing is shown until a group is large enough that no one can be identified

What you never see

An employer cannot see any individual dad's:

  • Check-ins, mood or journal entries
  • Scores or activity logs
  • Name against any result

Dads are told this in the app when they join with their company code.

How it works

  1. We talk

    A short call to understand your team and pick the right plan.

  2. We set you up

    Your company code, your dashboard and, on the branded plan, your look and feel.

  3. Dads join

    They download the app, enter your code and get Pro. Already a Dad Health member? Their existing account is added to your company's membership using the email they already use.

  4. You see the picture

    Anonymous team insights, updated as your dads use the app.

When a dad moves on, his progress goes with him

If someone leaves your company, he isn't left with nothing. He's offered the chance to move to his own personal Dad Health account and carry on, and we guide him through it in the app.

  1. We get in touch

    Your company lets us know when a dad leaves. We contact him, explain his options in plain English and talk about staying on.

  2. We walk him through it

    A few simple screens move him to a personal account. His score, streak and history come with him.

  3. He carries on

    He continues on his own subscription. If he'd rather not, he keeps a free account.

All we ask is that your company tells us when a dad leaves, so we can reach him in time. In return, his seat is freed up straight away, and a dad who has had a good experience leaves as an advocate for your company.
